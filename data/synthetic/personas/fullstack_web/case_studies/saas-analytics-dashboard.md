A saas client came to me because they needed to give customers self-serve usage reports. Working solo, I built a responsive dashboard with role-based views. Then I paginated and cached heavy report queries.

Outcome: it cut report load time from 9 s to 1.2 s. Main skills used: React, Next.js, TypeScript.
