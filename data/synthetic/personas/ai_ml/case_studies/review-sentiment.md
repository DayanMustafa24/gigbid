# App-review sentiment and topic mining

**Industry:** Mobile apps · **Team:** solo · **Year:** 2021

## Challenge
The client needed to understand why ratings dropped after a release.

## Approach
- Fine-tuned a transformer classifier on 25,000 labelled reviews.
- Grouped complaints into topics for the product team.

## Result
The work pinpointed the crash cause within a day and recovered a 0.4 star drop.
