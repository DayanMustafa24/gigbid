# Visual defect detection on a production line

A manufacturing client came to me because they needed to catch surface defects that human inspectors missed at line speed. Working solo, I trained an object detector on 9,000 annotated images. Then I exported the model for an edge device.

Outcome: it detected 94% of defects at 25 frames per second. Main skills used: computer vision, OpenCV.
